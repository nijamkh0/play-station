For alternative history fans

Valour Mortis

Release date: 10/13/26 ㅤ Genre: Action ㅤ Publisher: Lyrical Games

What would have happened if Napoléon won the Battle of Waterloo? According to
historians at Lyrical Games, Le Petit Caporal would have unleashed a supernatural
plague and plunged 19th century Europe into a zombie-ridden nightmare.

You must take up the cutlass of a resurrected Grande Armée soldier, fight unholy
abominations and uncover a grim conspiracy in this first-person soulslike.

For fans of games like: Elden Ring, Clair Obscur: Expedition 33

For survival horror lovers

Silver Pines

Release date: 10/26/08 ㅤ Genre: Horror ㅤ Publisher: Team 17

Why has musician Eddie Velvet disappeared in the town of Silver Pines? It’s your job
to find out in this 2D twist on the classic survival horror experience.

As private detective Red Walker, you’ll explore the eponymous town as you search for
clues and solve puzzles. Just make sure you keep an eye on your bullets and
medicine, because some of the area’s locals are less welcoming than others.

For fans of games like: Silent Hill 2, Alone in the Dark
